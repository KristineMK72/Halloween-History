The Night Between Worlds

Halloween is often told as pure pagan survival or pure commercial fun. The longer story is more layered — and more Christian than many people realize.

Christian toe-hold: For centuries the season has included All Saints’ Day, All Souls’ Day, church vigils, prayers for the dead, souling, and cemetery remembrance. Folk fire and modern costumes grew alongside that sacred calendar — not only instead of it.
